Word Trail

Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 Google Play Compliant COPPA & GDPR Aligned

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Word Trail (also referred to as "we", "our", or "us"). Word Trail is an engaging and relaxing word search puzzle game designed to challenge vocabulary, provide daily brain training, and offer hours of word-finding entertainment.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our application. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we may collect, how that information is used, and the choices available to you regarding your data.

Core Promise: We do NOT collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable personal information such as your name, email, phone number, address, or contact list.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Word Trail does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. As a result, we do not have access to or collect:

  • Your real name, postal address, or telephone number.
  • Your personal email address or passwords.
  • Your device contact lists, microphone audio, camera photos, or media files.
  • Your precise GPS location coordinates.
  • Financial account credentials or credit card numbers (all transactions, if any, are handled securely by Google Play Billing).

3. Information Collected Automatically & Third-Party Services

To support free gameplay and deliver high-quality performance, Word Trail integrates trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third parties may collect specific technical data as outlined below:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded video ads. In order to serve ads, prevent fraud, and verify impressions, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, and device hardware specifications (model, screen resolution, operating system version).
  • Network Information: IP address, mobile network carrier, and coarse (country or city level) location derived from IP.
  • Ad Telemetry: Ad impressions, clicks, video completion events, and diagnostic data.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

B. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

For users located in regions with applicable privacy frameworks (such as the European Economic Area / EEA and the United Kingdom under the GDPR / ePrivacy Directive), we display the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. This dialog allows you to grant or withhold consent for ad personalization and data usage.

4. On-Device Local Data Storage

Word Trail stores your gameplay progress directly and exclusively on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore and SharedPreferences. This includes:

  • Coins & Balances: Earned coins from completing puzzles and rewarded ads.
  • Puzzle Progress: Solved levels, star ratings (1, 2, or 3 stars), and unlocked word packs.
  • Daily Challenges: Solved calendar dates and daily streak counters.
  • App Preferences: Sound effects (on/off), haptic vibration (on/off), dark mode theme, and reminder notifications.

This data stays on your local device storage. You can erase this data at any time by tapping "Reset Game Progress" in the in-game Settings menu or by clearing the app's storage in your Android System Settings.

5. Android Permissions Explained

Word Trail requests only the minimum necessary permissions to function smoothly:

Permission Type Purpose
INTERNET Normal / Install-time Allows loading advertisements via Google AdMob and checking puzzle assets.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal / Install-time Monitors connection status to notify you if your network disconnects during gameplay.
VIBRATE Normal / Install-time Powers tactile haptic vibration when dragging letters and discovering words (toggleable in Settings).
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Allows sending daily puzzle reminders if you enable the Daily Reminder toggle in Settings.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Word Trail is designed as a family-friendly word search experience suitable for all general audiences. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • In-game ad requests are configured with Google AdMob's child-directed parameters (setTagForChildDirectedTreatment) where applicable to restrict behavioral advertising and profiling.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has inadvertently provided personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take corrective measures.

7. Your Privacy Choices & Rights

You have full control over your privacy while playing Word Trail:

  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID on your Android device by going to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based ads through Google's Ad Settings or via the UMP Privacy Options dialog inside Word Trail Settings.
  • Disable Notifications: You can toggle daily reminders off at any time in Word Trail Settings or via Android App Notification Settings.
  • Delete Game Data: Use the "Reset Game Progress" option in Settings to wipe all locally stored stats and progress.

8. Security

We value your trust and implement industry-standard practices to ensure your gameplay remains safe. Because Word Trail does not transmit personal data to external web servers, your exposure to unauthorized data access is minimized.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or Google Play policies. Any updates will be published with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
